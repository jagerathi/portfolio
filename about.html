Who I am

Leader, solutions architect, and developer.

I grew up with a passion for technology. I loved airplanes, space travel, and computers. When I reached the point where I had to decide what to do with my life, I quickly turned to technology. It started with a short stint at the Nokia Mobile Phones production facility in Fort Worth, followed by a year of managed IT services, and finally a software development role at Mik & Associates in Irving, TX.

I spent the next 10 years at Mik & Associates, and those years solidified my love for software development. I worked on countless custom projects across a variety of app spaces. I learned how to interact with customers, plan a project, convert that plan into an estimate, and the estimate into a real product. During this time the technology landscape changed: I moved from Access and Btrieve to SQL, and from C++ and MFC to C# and .NET.

The transition to .NET exposed me to many new frameworks, thanks to the rise of open source and the adaptation of popular Java frameworks. With these tools in hand I started to see the widespread adoption of industry standards. This was the stage of my career where I moved from developer to engineer, finally leveraging the technology knowledge from my earlier jobs into the solutions I was building.

After 10 years, I moved to ABC Imaging in Washington, DC. There my focus narrowed to a few core product ecosystems, which let me refine my craft. After two years I inherited a custom ERP system that would become my focus for the next decade-plus. It was built on .NET and SQL, but on an early framework and showing its age. Over a 10-year modernization cycle we took it from a monolith to an SOA and eventually to a cloud-based SaaS.

Part of that modernization was the DevOps process. We moved from SourceSafe to Subversion, then stood up a continuous build environment on TeamCity, then addressed deployment and configuration with Octopus Deploy. With those systems in place, the migration to AWS was expedited — and we completed it in under a year.
